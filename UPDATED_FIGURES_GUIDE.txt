HOW TO INSERT IN WORD DOCUMENT
===================================================================

**Step 1**: Open your KDD paper Word document

**Step 2**: Find "Model Training Methodology" section (Page 3)

**Step 3**: After this section, add new heading:
   "Training Process Analysis: Individual Model Learning"

**Step 4**: For EACH individual figure, do this:
   a) Insert PNG image (Insert > Pictures)
   b) Add figure caption: "Figure X: [Model Name] Training Process"
   c) The explanation is ALREADY in the figure at the bottom
   d) You can also copy explanation from INDIVIDUAL_FIGURES_EXPLANATIONS.txt
      and paste as separate paragraph if you want

**Step 5**: Insert overview figures (training_progression, convergence_analysis, etc.)

**Step 6**: Update Results section with APA-formatted figures

**Step 7**: Save and review
